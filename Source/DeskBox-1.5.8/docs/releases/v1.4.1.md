# DeskBox 1.4.1

DeskBox 1.4.1 is a focused reliability and interface-polish update. File-widget scrollbars now stay hidden until they are needed, and Hide All no longer allows a delayed desktop-layer callback to reopen widgets after the user hides them.

## Highlights

- **Scrollbars appear only during activity.** File-widget grid and list layouts reveal their vertical scrollbar during pointer, wheel, or keyboard interaction, then hide it after three seconds without input.
- **Hide All keeps widgets hidden.** DeskBox cancels pending automatic and topmost safety restoration as hiding begins.
- **Stale restoration is blocked centrally.** Hidden, actively hiding, and closing windows reject delayed desktop-layer restoration, covering both ordinary content widgets and Quick Capture.
- **Lifecycle cleanup is complete.** Scrollbar timers stop when their file surface is unloaded or disposed.

## 中文说明

DeskBox 1.4.1 是一次稳定性与界面细节更新。文件格子的滚动条仅在需要时出现；用户执行“全部收起”后，延迟到达的桌面层恢复回调也不会再把格子重新显示。

## 更新亮点

- **滚动条按操作显示。** 文件格子的图标和列表布局会在鼠标移动、滚轮或键盘操作时显示纵向滚动条，连续 3 秒无输入后自动隐藏。
- **全部收起后保持隐藏。** 隐藏开始时，DeskBox 会取消尚未执行的自动恢复和置顶安全恢复。
- **统一阻止旧恢复回调。** 已隐藏、正在收起或关闭中的窗口会拒绝延迟桌面层恢复，普通内容格子与随记窗口都已覆盖。
- **定时器生命周期完整。** 文件界面卸载或释放时会停止滚动条定时器。
